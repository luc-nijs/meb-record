Record a short message — your voice, or yourself on camera. Voice messages can be up to 90 seconds, video up to 60.

Before you record

Your message may be published. If we choose yours, it will appear on the Music Education Bites pages of mutechlab.education, where anyone can watch or listen to it. We select which messages to publish — not all are published.

What we keep: the recording, the date, and which topic it was about. We do not ask for or store your name, your email address, or your IP address.

You can change your mind. When you send your message you will be shown a short reference code. Keep it. Email luc.nijs@uni.lu with that code and we will delete your recording, including from the website if it is already there. You do not need to give a reason.

How long we keep it. Published recordings stay online while the project runs. Recordings we do not publish are deleted within 3 months.

Please record only yourself. Make sure no one else can be seen or heard — in particular, no children.

Your browser will ask permission for the microphone or camera. Nothing is sent until you press send.
